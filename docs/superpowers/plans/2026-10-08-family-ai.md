# Family initiative

Goal: AI factions participate in domestic marriage and diplomatic marriage under player-equivalent rules.

Architecture: pure `familyInitiative` action selection, no direct mutation. Stagger once per 17 turns by faction index, skip turn zero, player faction and treasury below 120. Prefer valid domestic spouse unions, prioritizing rulers. Otherwise propose peace/alliance marriage with gift 40 only when validator and existing diplomatic score accept. Resolvers retain player inbox/escrow semantics. Integrate rule and learned hybrid pipelines after urgent diplomacy; explicitly not learned romantic behavior.
