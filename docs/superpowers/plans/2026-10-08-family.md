# Family relationships plan

Implement persisted unions with spouse/concubine kinds and original sandbox adult ages (30, not claimed historical ages). Both participants must be free adult members of the issuing faction, loyal >=50, not commanding, unrelated by recorded parents, and different surname (conservative clan restriction). Marriage allows one principal spouse per person; concubinage requires male household head and female partner, with at most three concubines. No participant may join multiple households. Cost 40/60, loyalty +5. All actions use existing one-order-per-turn validation and immutable resolution.
