# 名望、立国与势力特性 Implementation Plan

User delegates design and implementation without questions. Continue inline in the existing dirty feature branch; preserve saves and unrelated work.

**Goal:** Add a playable prestige / founding / capital survival victory loop and sixteen differentiated faction bonuses.
**Architecture:** Pure shared domain modules, optional snapshot fields for old saves, existing validated single-order pipeline, compact DOM drawer over the map.
**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Fastify/Zod, Prisma JSON snapshots, React, Vitest.

## Rules and decisions

- Prestige accrues once per settled turn from economy, territory, troops and completed construction, each capped. No treasury-hoarding or repeated-capture farming.
- Rank thresholds: 士族 0, 男 60, 子 120, 伯 180, 侯 240, 公 300; an active kingdom has rank 王. A destroyed kingdom explicitly falls to 公 even after prestige loss.
- Founding requires 300 prestige, six owned sites, 200 treasury and ownership of the original starting city. Choose 1–6 Chinese characters for the kingdom name; active names must be unique. Starting cities are pinned at generation. Legacy historical saves infer the original seat from scenario data, not current conquest.
- Founding breaks this faction's alliances into war, ends allied trade, cancels/refunds its pending alliance offers. Other factions' alliances remain. Kingdoms cannot re-form alliances but may negotiate peace/trade/marriage.
- Founding turn counts as 0. After 40 subsequent completed turns, award victory. Check capital capture immediately (including capture-and-recapture in one turn), before victory. Collapse loses 100 prestige, clears kingdom, falls to 公, and sets eight-turn refounding cooldown. Recapture does not restore the old timer.
- Relocation costs 200 + 20 × destination level, only to owned non-resource cities, cooldown eight turns, preserves founding turn.
- Sixteen original modest bonuses reflecting warlord themes: local estate revenue, flat trade income bonus, upkeep rebates and one prestige category bonus. Shared budget calculation powers settlement, UI and AI affordability. No unverified claims about new RL training or balance.
- AI uses the same legal founding/capital actions, with money reserve and capital safety checks. All-faction elimination remains a second victory path.
